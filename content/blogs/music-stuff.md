Excerpt from the solo violin part of Antonio Vivaldi’s Violin Concerto in A minor, RV 356, I. Allegro.

This is probably my favourite warm up piece before I practice. I usually play the opening part of the Allegro, skip over some repetitive middle section then towards the final bit. It may not be the most popular warm up piece, but somehow I keep coming back to it. Vivaldi has been one of my favourite composers. I also played this concerto at an end of year school show, that was exciting. I played solo, and it was my first time playing in front of audiences. It felt quite different when playing to myself and playing under a spotlight with all the attention and hundreds of eyes on me. At the time I had recently moved to my new 4/4 violin for a short while, and its “debut” went well I guess. Although I never knew how exciting or boring the other schoolchildren found Vivaldi, most of them probably preferred something less Baroque.
