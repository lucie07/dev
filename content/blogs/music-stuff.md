Excerpt from the solo violin part of Antonio Vivaldi’s *Violin Concerto in A minor, RV 356,* I. Allegro.

This is probably my favourite warm up piece before I practice. I usually play the opening part of the Allegro, skip over some repetitive middle section then towards the final bit. It may not be the most popular warm up piece, but somehow I keep coming back to it. Vivaldi has been one of my favourite composers. I also played this concerto at an end of year school show, that was exciting. I played solo, and it was my first time playing in front of audiences. It felt quite different when playing to myself and playing under a spotlight with all the attention and hundreds of eyes on me. At the time I had recently moved to my new 4/4 violin for a short while, and its “debut” went well I guess. Although I never knew how exciting or boring the other schoolchildren found Vivaldi, most of them probably preferred something less Baroque. 

![Antique violin](blogs-img/antique-violin.jpg)

The unnamed.

The unnamed. This piece of antique has been with me for over twenty years. I can't think of anything else I've owned for more than two decades in my life. I got it when I was ten, after my violin teacher told my mum that my arms had grown long enough for a full-size instrument. So I welcomed my brand-new “unnamed”. It was my first 4/4 violin, and my previous 1/2 and 1/4 violins were officially retired and locked in the deepest of our storeroom since then. The unnamed was of pretty good quality, possibly made locally in Inner Mongolia, although the details of its make and model have sadly been lost. It survived through the years, and I still love playing it. I never gave it a name, though. It has a scratch on its left side, which I suspect happened one day when I was in a hurry to get to dinner after finishing my last piece of practice.. the violin ended up with a permanent scar.
